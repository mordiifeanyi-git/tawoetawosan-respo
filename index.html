Our Practice Expertise & Core Domains

The firm's practice covers several broad legal areas, delivering specialized, optimum counsel across complex commercial and municipal law sectors.

Litigation, Advocacy & Arbitration

  • Commercial and criminal litigation, including debt recovery
  • Commencing and defending court actions and legal proceedings
  • Arbitration, mediation, and reconciliation

Commercial Law & Business Advisory

  • Comprehensive legal drafting and property documentation
  • Loan agreements, syndication, guarantees, and equipment leasing
  • Company formation, joint ventures, mergers, and acquisitions
  • Escrow agency, cross-border trade, and investment advisory

Admiralty

  • Maritime and shipping law, vessel registration, and P&I
  • International sale and trade, charter parties, and syndication
  • Vessel acquisitions, cargo claims, mortgages, and arrests

Aviation

  • Routine advisory services and dispute settlement (litigation)
  • Charter parties and international carriage of goods
  • Banking, finance, and aircraft-related mortgages

Mining, Energy & Natural Resources

  • Oil and gas law, power purchase & franchise agreements
  • Grid connection agreements and NERC/TCN licensing
  • Mining leases, licenses, and extraction consultancy
  • Mining-related tax issues and project finance options
