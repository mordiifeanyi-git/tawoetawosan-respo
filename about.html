ABOUT US

Tawo E. Tawo, SAN & Co., is a premier firm of Legal Practitioners, consultants, Notary Public and Arbitrators incorporated on the 13th of April, 1989 under the Laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Driven by an unwavering commitment to optimum professionalism and strict ethical standards, our operations fully accord with international best practices.

Our practice covers a robust spectrum of law, including legal drafting, litigation, alternative dispute resolution (arbitration), energy, oil and gas, construction documentation, debt recovery, criminal laws, capital markets, and tax laws.
