ABOUT US

Tawo E. Tawo, SAN & Co., is a premier firm of Legal Practitioners, consultants, Notary Public and Arbitrators incorporated on the 13th of April, 1989 under the Laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Driven by an unwavering commitment to optimum professionalism and strict ethical standards, our operations fully accord with exceptional international practices.

Our practice covers a robust spectrum of law, including legal drafting, litigation, alternative dispute resolution (arbitration), energy, oil and gas, construction documentation, debt recovery, criminal laws, capital markets, and tax laws.

The Principal Partner and founder of the firm, Tawo E. Tawo, SAN, MCIARB(UK), is a Senior Advocate of Nigeria, member of the chattered institute of Arbitrators(UK) and a Notary public with upwards of thirty two (32) years post call to bar experience.

This firm maintains Offices in Abuja, Yenagoa, Lagos, Calabar and a Liaison in London. UK. With a knack for optimum, professionalism and right ethical standard that accord with international best practices.
