Principal Partner

Principal Partner / Founder

Chief Tawo Eja Tawo, SAN

FICMC, MCIArb.(UK)

Chief Tawo E. Tawo, SAN is a distinguished Senior Advocate of Nigeria, Notary Public, and esteemed member of the Body of Benchers. A graduate of the University of Lagos, he was called to the Bar in 1987 before founding Tawo E. Tawo, SAN & Co. in 1991.

A Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Mediators and Conciliators and a Member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (UK), his extensive expertise covers commercial litigation, receivership, liquidations, maritime law, oil and gas, and arbitration. He currently serves as Chairman of the National Panel of the Legal Practitioner Disciplinary Committee of the Nigerian Bar Association.

Alongside memberships in the International Bar Association and African Bar Association, Chief Tawo is a dedicated philanthropist and Founder of the Resolute Law Foundation, advancing legal practice and community standards.
